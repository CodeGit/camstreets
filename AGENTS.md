then invoke the nextjs-doc skill for documentation.


## Writing style (docs, code comments, commit messages)

- Use a technical term when it's the most precise way to say something
  (migration, RLS policy, trigger, subquery). Otherwise use plain English.
- Avoid business jargon and consultant-speak - use the ordinary word:
  "release" not "rollout", "shown" not "surfaced", "the full list" not "the
  source of truth", "copied" not "snapshotted".
- Use UK spelling ("honour", "materialise", "colour").
- Prefer short sentences and say what something does, not how good it is.
